Gym Scribe

Workout Log & Planner, for iPhone

Gym Scribe replaces the notebook you carry to the gym. It plans a cycle of workouts, usually five, and moves on only when you finish one, so a missed day never throws the plan off. Log sets as you go, and it suggests your next weights.

Common questions

Where are my workouts stored?

Only on your iPhone. Gym Scribe has no accounts and no servers, and it never sends your data anywhere. Your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup includes it.

How do I move my history to a new phone?

Restoring the new iPhone from a backup of the old one brings Gym Scribe's history with it. You can also go to Settings › Data › Export backup on the old phone, send the file to yourself, and use Restore backup on the new one.

How does the cycle work?

The app lays out every workout in the cycle up front, so each exercise gets its turn before anything repeats. The next workout is always the one after the last one you finished, whatever day it is. You can swap an exercise for the day or change how the cycle is built in Settings.

The rest timer alert doesn't appear

Check that notifications are allowed in the iPhone Settings app, under Notifications › Gym Scribe. The rest timer itself can be changed or switched off in Gym Scribe's Settings › Workout.

How do I save workouts to Apple Health?

Switch on Apple Health in Settings › Preferences. Gym Scribe saves each finished workout to Health. It never reads your Health data.

Can I use the app sideways?

It stays upright by default. Turn on Allow rotation in Settings › Preferences to use it in landscape.

How do I delete everything?

Settings › Data › Erase all data removes it all from the app. Deleting the app does the same. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app.
